2. Visiting this website

Hosting: This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which processes your IP address and technical access data to deliver the website securely (GitHub's privacy statement).

Visitor statistics: We use Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) on our behalf to count visits. According to Cloudflare, it does not use cookies to track visitors. You can block it with a content blocker (Cloudflare's privacy policy).

Display setting: The website follows the light or dark setting of your device. If you click the light/dark switch, your choice is stored in your own browser for the current session only, under the name "diafane-mode". It is deleted when you close the browser. Nothing is stored unless you click the switch.

Legal basis under the GDPR: our legitimate interest in a secure website and in knowing how it is used.

3. Contacting us

The forms on this website send nothing to us; they only prepare an email in your own email program. If you write to us or talk to us, we process the information you give us, for example your name, company, role, email address and message, and the notes we take in conversations. Purpose: to answer you, hold conversations and understand the needs of quality professionals while we develop DiafaneAI.

Recipients: our email provider Infomaniak Network SA, Switzerland.

Providing these data is voluntary; without them, we cannot reply to you. Legal basis under the GDPR: our legitimate interest in answering you and learning from quality professionals.

4. If we contact you first

We may contact quality professionals whose professional details we obtained from public professional sources or through recommendations. We use your name, employer, job title and contact details to ask whether you would like to talk to us. Legal basis under the GDPR: our legitimate interest in learning from quality professionals.

5. Updates

We only send you email updates if you have asked for them. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or writing to contact@diafaneai.com. Legal basis under the GDPR: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future.

6. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as we need them for the purposes described in this statement, and your email address for updates until you unsubscribe.

7. Disclosure abroad

GitHub and Cloudflare are based in the USA and may also process data in other countries worldwide. Both are certified under the Swiss-U.S. and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; Switzerland and the European Commission recognise an adequate level of data protection for certified companies. For other countries, they use the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission.

8. Your rights

You have the right to request information about your data, and to have it corrected, deleted or its processing restricted. You can object to processing, withdraw your consent at any time and receive data you gave us in a common electronic format. Write to contact@diafaneai.com. You can report a breach of data protection law to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). If the GDPR applies to you, you can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in the EU/EEA.

Right to object: Where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interests, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation by writing to contact@diafaneai.com.
